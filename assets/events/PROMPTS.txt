EVENT-SFEERBEELDEN
===================
Eén liggend sfeerbeeld per event (PNG of JPG, ~1024x576), getoond
als groot OMKADERD PANEEL boven de eventtekst — het beeld komt dus
nooit achter tekst te staan. Personages mógen vol in beeld (de
smid, de koopman...). Bestandsnaam = event-id.

LET OP: het paneel snijdt de boven- en onderrand van een
16:9-beeld bij (het venster is een bredere band). Houd het
onderwerp dus VERTICAAL GECENTREERD; zet niets belangrijks
helemaal boven- of onderaan.

SJABLOON (vervang alleen de Scene-regel):

  Painterly dark fantasy scene illustration for a deckbuilder
  game event, hand-painted concept art, dramatic torchlight,
  palette of deep purples, charcoal and ember orange. NOT
  photorealistic, no photo, no 3D render. Landscape, cinematic
  composition, no text, no frame, no UI.

  Scene: [BESCHRIJVING]

koopman.png   (De Zwervende Koopman — een mens OPGEGETEN door het systeem/archief)
  Scene: a hollow wandering HUSK of a former 198X corporate office worker, now half-
  CONSUMED by the bureaucratic archive — gaunt, paper-grey, hollow-eyed, with bundles
  of contracts, curled ledgers, punch-cards, red-tape and rusted index-tabs GROWING
  THROUGH and out of his body like a parasitic fungus (erupting from his collar, cuffs
  and ribs), his lower half trailing off into a drift of loose papers and filing-
  drawers — he is slowly BECOMING part of the archive. He wears the rotted remnants of
  an OFFICE SUIT (frayed blazer, limp tie) under the paper-growth, and on his chest a
  faded 198X plastic EMPLOYEE ID BADGE on a lanyard reading "B.A.A.S. — MEDEWERKER",

  and a barcode, the corporate logo defaced and ink-bled — the last trace of who he
  was, now indexed and filed away. He OPENS his tattered coat toward the viewer to
  reveal rows of glinting POTION VIALS tucked among the files he is fused with — still
  doing business, hollow and rote, a vacant pleading half-smile, one still-human eye
  catching the warm torchlight. Tragic corporate-horror, uncanny and pitiable — a
  cautionary mirror of the rat race. Vertically centred.
  (Compleet ander personage dan de winkelier/klerk-koopman: die is een sluwe levende
  fence; DIT is een tragisch, half-opgegeten slachtoffer van het systeem.)

============= ACT 2 — HET ARCHIEF =============
De 4 nieuwe Act 2-EVENTS (Het Onafgewerkte Dossier, De Kopieermachine,
De Naamloze Klerk, Het Verloren Origineel) staan met hun prompts én
bouw-data gebundeld in ACT2-PROMPTBIB.md (repo-root), sectie "4. Events".

De 2 FIGUUR-events hieronder (Act 2+) horen bij het Drops-mysterie en geven de
drops_figuur-scherf. Elk een eigen onderscheidend beeld:

lantaarndrager.png   (FIGUUR-event, Act 2+ — De Gedoofde Lantaarndrager)
  Scene: a tall gaunt CLOAKED wanderer deep in a pitch-dark archive aisle, holding
  ALOFT a wrought-iron LANTERN whose flame is DEAD and OUT — cold black glass, a
  single thread of grey dead-smoke and one faint, almost-spent BLUE ember barely
  glowing inside it. His face is lost in the hood; he feels his way through the
  unlit niches, untroubled by the dark as if he belongs to it. Almost everything is
  swallowed in blackness — the dark lantern is the focal point: a lantern-bearer who
  carries NO light. Mournful, spectral, ominous, a herald of the doused flame. Cold
  near-black palette with that one dying blue ember as the only glow. Vertically centred.

spiegelaar.png   (FIGUUR-event, Act 2+ — De Naamloze Spiegelaar)
  Scene: a gaunt, hollow WRAITH-figure standing beside a tall cracked STANDING-MIRROR
  in a cold archive alcove — but he himself has NO FACE: where his face should be is
  smooth BLANK glass / blank parchment, nameless and identity-less. The mirror does
  not reflect HIM; instead its cracked surface throws back a faint warped reflection
  of the VIEWER (the hero) with pale duplicate selves fading behind. He reaches one
  thin hand toward the glass, mournful, longing for a name he no longer has. Cold
  silver-grey mirror light, hairline cracks, one small warm torch-glint on a shard.
  Tragic, eerie, identity-less — a THIN lost figure, NOT an armoured guardian (≠ de
  spiegelwachter-vijand). Vertically centred.
