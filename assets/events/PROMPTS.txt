EVENT-SFEERBEELDEN
===================
Eén liggend sfeerbeeld per event (PNG of JPG, ~1024x576), getoond
als groot OMKADERD PANEEL boven de eventtekst — het beeld komt dus
nooit achter tekst te staan. Personages mógen vol in beeld (de
smid, de koopman...). Bestandsnaam = event-id.

LET OP: het paneel snijdt de boven- en onderrand van een
16:9-beeld bij (het venster is een bredere band). Houd het
onderwerp dus VERTICAAL GECENTREERD; zet niets belangrijks
helemaal boven- of onderaan.

SJABLOON (vervang alleen de Scene-regel):

  Painterly dark fantasy scene illustration for a deckbuilder
  game event, hand-painted concept art, dramatic torchlight,
  palette of deep purples, charcoal and ember orange. NOT
  photorealistic, no photo, no 3D render. Landscape, cinematic
  composition, no text, no frame, no UI.

  Scene: [BESCHRIJVING]

DE SCENES:

altaar.png
  Scene: an ancient black stone altar in a dungeon alcove, dried
  blood in its carved grooves, a faint whispering purple glow
  rising from it, candles guttering around its base.

smid.png
  Scene: a weathered old blacksmith at a glowing forge deep in a
  cave, hammer resting on an anvil, sparks drifting, weapon racks
  in the shadows behind him.

vonkaltaar.png
  Scene: a low ancient stone shrine-altar in a dark cavern, a single
  flame burning above it that consumes no fuel — cold blue at its
  edges, hungry white-gold at its heart, casting flickering light on
  worn carved runes; faint embers and sparks drifting upward, no
  blacksmith, no figure — just the eerie living flame waiting. Mysterious,
  sacred, slightly ominous (a gamble of fire). Keep the flame vertically
  centered.

kist.png
  Scene: a single ornate wooden chest standing suspiciously alone
  in the middle of a torchlit dungeon corridor, faint scratch
  marks on the floor around it.

fontein.png
  Scene: a gothic stone fountain bubbling with dark crimson
  liquid, faint red mist drifting over its rim, candles and bones
  scattered at its base.

koopman.png
  Scene: a mysterious merchant in ragged robes opening his coat
  in a dark archway, dozens of glowing potion vials glinting
  inside the lining, one eye catching the torchlight.

vuurvliegjes.png
  Scene: a swarm of golden-green fireflies dancing through a dark
  dungeon gallery, their tiny lights reflecting on wet stone.

avonturier.png
  Scene: a fallen adventurer slumped against a dungeon wall,
  backpack spilled open, a bulging coin pouch at his hip, his
  empty eyes catching a sliver of torchlight.
