ACT 2 — DE KOPIEERHEL · GENEREER-KLARE VIJAND-PROMPTS
======================================================
Eén zelfstandig blok per wezen: de stijl-prefix zit er al IN, plus de pose-set
+ death-flavor. Kopieer gewoon één heel blok in je generator. Volgorde per wezen:
maak de BASIS, en in DEZELFDE sessie (zelfde wezen, alleen de "Pose:"-regel anders)
de poses. Bestandsnaam = exact de id (bv. echo.png, echo_death.png).

PIJPLIJN: cut-outs → dambord-pijplijn. UITZONDERINGEN: echo + spiegelwachter staan
op EFFEN ZWARTE achtergrond (doorschijnend/glas) → aparte verwerking.
Volledige bron + differentiatie-tabel: zie PROMPTS.txt (deze blokken zijn identiek).

Painterly dark-fantasy concept art, no photo / no 3D render, expressive brushwork. Single full-body
creature facing LEFT on a plain solid BLACK background (translucent figure — no transparency), fills
~90% of a 1024x1024 canvas with margin. Cold blue-grey Archief "system light" + a thin warm ember rim.
Theme: forgery/copy.
Character: a ghostly humanoid with NO single solid body — three or four translucent duplicate
silhouettes of the SAME figure stacked and slightly OFFSET like a frozen after-image, each layer
fainter, edges glowing faint VIOLET-WHITE; made of cold light, not flesh. You can't tell which layer
is the real one.
  echo (basis)  — Pose: reaching forward, the echo-layers trailing behind the gesture.
  echo_death    — Pose: the offset after-image layers peel apart and wink out one by one into nothing.
