STATE-POSES — PROMPTBIBLIOTHEEK
================================
BELANGRIJK: voeg bij ELKE generatie de basisafbeelding van het
personage toe als referentie (speler.png / gifmagier.png /
slijmkoning.png uit deze map) — dat is dé sleutel tot consistentie.
Genereer per personage in één sessie. Opslaan onder exact de
bestandsnaam boven het blok, droppen in assets/karakters/, en bij
dambord gewoon "ruim op" vragen.

Helden kijken naar RECHTS, de baas naar LINKS — net als hun basis.

1024x1024 canvas.

A weathered tiefling druid with brick-red skin covered in dark
swirling tattoos. Two large curling ram horns, long wild black hair
and a full black beard. A black leather eyepatch covers his right eye
(the eye facing the viewer); his remaining eye glows ember-orange.
A long red tail. His left lower leg is living carved wood with clawed
wooden toes; his right foot is a dark cloven hoof. He wears earthy
brown travel clothes, a leather chest harness, one bandaged forearm,
a ragged moss-green hooded half-cape and a deep-green embroidered
apron with gold thread; a small copper cooking pot, herb pouches and
wooden vials hang from his belt. In his hand a tall gnarled wooden
staff wrapped in twine and moss, with a knot of SMOLDERING ORANGE
COALS glowing at its heart. Confident crooked grin. Palette: warm
russet skin, charcoal black hair, forest green and earth brown
clothing, ember-orange glow as the light accent.
